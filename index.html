The problem

Inventors get stuck before the first partner conversation

Most OEM outreach assumes cash for tooling and MOQs. Royalty- and joint-dev-friendly factories exist — they’re just hard to find and harder to approach cleanly.

Upfront tooling walls

Traditional OEM quotes demand tooling cash before trust. Inventors with IP — not spare capital — stall out.

Alibaba is noisy. Finding factories open to royalty or joint-dev takes operator judgment, not a generic search.

Outreach without structure

Cold messages without a one-pager, NDA path, or royalty frame burn goodwill and invite IP leakage.
